3D Scan Analysis & Dimensional Inspection

Overview

The objective of this experiment is to inspect dimensional and geometrical tolerances, create a surface comparison, and subsequently derive suitable measures to eliminate or reduce occurring errors using a generated 3D scan within the GOM Inspect software. In the first step, the test object and its manufacturing method are explained. Subsequently, the GOM Inspect software is introduced in more detail before describing the experimental procedure step by step.
